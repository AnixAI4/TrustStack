## Category 4: Certifications

### International (Relevant)
- IAPP CIPP/E, CIPM, CIPT

- ISO 27701 Lead Implementer/Auditor
- CISA, CISSP

### Indian (Emerging)

- CERT-In Empanelled Auditor — cybersecurity only

### BIGGEST CERTIFICATION GAPS
- NO government-recognized DPDP certification exists
- No Certified DPDP Professional equivalent
- No DPO certification recognized by Data Protection Board
- No DPDP Auditor certification
- No company-level DPDP compliance badge

- Breach Response-as-a-Service — not DPDP-specific

- Privacy Operations-as-a-Service — completely absent
