# Step 1: What is DPDP? — The Full Picture

## The Story That Started It All

Imagine you walk into a hospital. You fill a form with your name, phone number, Aadhaar number, medical history. You trust the hospital with this data.

Now imagine that hospital sells your data to an insurance company. That insurance company denies you a policy because of your medical history. You never gave permission. You never even knew.

This happened. Millions of times. In India. Every single day.

Now imagine a bank shares your loan history with a marketing firm. A school sells student data to ed-tech companies. An e-commerce app tracks your location 24/7 even when you're not using it.

India had no law to stop any of this. Until now.

---

## The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)

- **Passed:** August 11, 2023
- **Presidential assent:** August 11, 2023
- **Rules:** Being finalized — expected notification in 2025-2026
- **Enforcement:** Expected 2026+

---

## The Cast of Characters

| Role | Who They Are | Real World Example |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| Data Fiduciary | Company holding your data | Hospital, Bank, App, School |
| Data Principal | The person whose data it is | Every Indian citizen |
| Data Processor | IT vendor processing on behalf of Fiduciary | Cloud provider, SaaS vendor |
| Data Protection Board of India | The Regulator | India's new powerful watchdog |

---

## The 7 Core Principles

| # | Principle | Plain English |
|---|-----------|--------------|
| 1 | Lawful Purpose | Only collect data for a specific, clear reason |
| 2 | Consent | Must ask permission — no buried checkboxes |
| 3 | Data Minimisation | Collect ONLY what you need |
| 4 | Data Accuracy | Keep it correct and updated |
| 5 | Storage Limitation | Delete it when the purpose is done |
| 6 | Security | Protect with proper safeguards |
| 7 | Accountability | Company is RESPONSIBLE if anything goes wrong |

---

## Rights of Every Indian Citizen

- Right to know — what data a company holds
- Right to correct — fix wrong data
- Right to erase — "Right to be Forgotten"
- Right to grievance — complain and get a response
- Right to nominate — assign someone to act on your behalf

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Maximum Fine |
|-----------|-------------|
| Data breach due to no security safeguards | ₹250 Crore |
| Not notifying a breach | ₹200 Crore |
| Violation of children's data provisions | ₹200 Crore |
| Non-compliance with Data Protection Board orders | ₹150 Crore |
| Any other violation | ₹50 Crore |

---

## DPDP vs GDPR

| Feature | GDPR (EU) | DPDP (India) |
|---------|-----------|--------------|
| Year | 2018 | 2023 |
| Max Penalty | €20M or 4% global revenue | ₹250 Crore |
| DPO Required | Yes (for some) | DPO equivalent in Rules |
| Children's Age | Under 16 | Under 18 |
| Extraterritorial | Yes | Yes |

## Timeline

- 2023 Aug — DPDP Act Passed
- 2024 — Rules drafted, public consultations
- 2025 — Rules expected to be notified
- 2025-26 — Enforcement begins
- 2026+ — Full enforcement, penalties start

## Key Insight

DPDP is India's GDPR moment. GDPR created a $5 Billion+ compliance industry globally.
DPDP will do the same in India at 10x the scale — 1.4 billion people, 750M+ internet users,
almost zero compliance infrastructure today.

**The gap between what's required and what exists = Your business opportunity.**
