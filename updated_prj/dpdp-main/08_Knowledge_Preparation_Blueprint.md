# Step 7: Knowledge & Preparation Blueprint

## The 5 Knowledge Domains

1. Legal & Regulatory Knowledge
2. Technology & Data Architecture
3. Business & Consulting Frameworks
4. Industry-Specific Knowledge
5. Business Building & GTM Knowledge

---

## Domain 1: Legal & Regulatory

### Must Know Cold (Week 1-2)
- DPDP Act 2023 — full 40-page Act (MeitY website)
- Key sections: 4,5,6,7,9,10,11,12-14,17,33
- IT Act Sections 43A, 72A
- IT Rules 2011 SPDI Rules
- CERT-In Directions 2022

### Must Understand (Week 3-4)
- GDPR basics (for client comparisons)
- RBI IT Framework for Banks
- RBI Digital Lending Guidelines 2022
- SEBI Cybersecurity Circular 2023
- IRDAI Cyber Security Guidelines 2023
- ABDM Health Data Management Policy

### Hire For Legal
- Senior Privacy Lawyer (ex-Big Law) — ₹30-50L/year or ₹2-5L/month retainer
- Regulatory Affairs Specialist (ex-RBI/SEBI/MeitY) — ₹20-40L/year
- GOLDMINE: Retired IAS officer who served in MeitY/DPBI setup

## Domain 2: Technology & Data Architecture

### Must Know (2 weeks)
- Data inventory / data map concept
- Data lineage, classification (Standard/Sensitive/Children's)
- PII definition
- Pseudonymization vs anonymization (key DPDP distinction)
- How consent management platforms work
- Consent receipts, cookie categories, double opt-in
- Encryption at rest vs in transit
- Data residency vs data sovereignty
- AWS/Azure/GCP India regions

### Hire For Tech
- CTO/VP Engineering — ₹40-80L/year (SaaS experience)
- Data Architect/Privacy Engineer — ₹25-45L/year
- Cybersecurity Expert (CISSP) — ₹20-40L/year

### Tech Stack
- Frontend: React.js / Vue.js
- Backend: Node.js / Python
- Database: PostgreSQL + MongoDB
- Cloud: AWS India (ap-south-1 Mumbai)
- Security: ISO 27001 certified from Day 1

## Domain 3: Business & Consulting Frameworks

### Must Learn
- NIST Privacy Framework (free PDF)
- ISO 27701:2019 (get Lead Implementer certified)
- DSCI Privacy Framework (India-specific)
- Data Privacy Maturity Model (5 levels)

### The 6-Phase DPDP Delivery Framework
1. Assess — Gap analysis and data inventory
2. Design — Privacy program blueprint
3. Implement — Policies, processes, technology
4. Train — Awareness and capability building
5. Audit — Verify and certify compliance
6. Monitor — Ongoing compliance management

### Must-Read Books
- "Privacy by Design" — Ann Cavoukian
- "EU GDPR — A Practical Guide" — Paul Voigt
- "The Challenger Sale" — Matthew Dixon (for enterprise sales)
- "From Impossible to Inevitable" — Aaron Ross

## Domain 4: Industry-Specific Knowledge

### BFSI (Learn First — 2 weeks)
- RBI Master Directions on IT Framework
- RBI Digital Lending Guidelines 2022
- Account Aggregator framework
- KYC + Aadhaar-based eKYC process
- PMLA data retention requirements
- SEBI LODR Regulations

### Healthcare (Learn Second — 1 week)
- ABDM full framework (abdm.gov.in)
- ABHA ID data implications
- Electronic Health Records Standards
- Telemedicine Practice Guidelines 2020
- IRDAI health insurance data guidelines

### Edtech (Learn Third — 3-4 days)
- DPDP Section 9 (children's data) in detail
- COPPA (US benchmark for children's privacy)
- GDPR Article 8 (EU benchmark)
- UGC Online Education Regulations

## Domain 5: Business Building & GTM

### Must Know
- Value-based pricing (not cost-plus)
- SaaS pricing models
- Enterprise vs mid-market vs SME sales cycles
- Channel partner / reseller model
- Government tender process (GeM, RFP, L1 bidding)

### Government Registrations (Day 1)
- GeM portal registration (gem.gov.in)
- Startup India (startupindia.gov.in)
- MSME Udyam registration
- NSIC registration
- Company incorporation (Private Limited)
