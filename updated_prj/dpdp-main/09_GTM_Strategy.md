3. DPDP Penalty Calculator — "Your exposure = ₹X Crore" → call to action

Target: 5,000 leads/month from free tools. Cost to build: ₹5-10 Lakh (one-time).

- Weekly Monday 8AM email
- Growth: LinkedIn + YouTube + NASSCOM/DSCI cross-promotion
- Monetization: Sponsored slots (₹50K-2L/issue), premium membership (₹2,999/year)

## Engine 2: Direct Enterprise Sales

### Target List: Top 500 companies
- BFSI: 50 banks + 50 NBFCs + 30 insurance
- Healthcare: 50 hospital chains + 20 health-tech
- Edtech: 30 platforms + 20 school chains
- Tools: LinkedIn Sales Navigator (₹50K/year), Apollo.io

### Approach Sequence
| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| 1 | Personalized insight email — "Your ₹X Crore DPDP exposure" |
| 5 | Fear email — attach 1-page industry DPDP risk brief |
| 10 | Social proof email — similar company case study |
| 12 | LinkedIn message |
| 15 | Warm phone call |

### Discovery Meeting Framework
Ask in order:
1. Current DPDP understanding (calibrate)
2. Has legal done gap assessment? (usually NO)
3. How do you manage customer consent? (usually no answer)
4. How handling RBI + DPDP dual obligation? (pain point)
5. Timeline for compliance? (urgency)
6. Who else involved in decision? (buying committee)
Goal: DIAGNOSE, not sell. End with request for deeper engagement.

### Proposal Structure
1. Executive Summary — "Your risk = ₹X Crore"
2. Current State findings
3. Gap Analysis
4. Phased Solution
5. Timeline (30/60/90 day milestones)
6. Investment (3 options: Basic/Recommended/Premium)
7. Team credentials
8. Case Studies
9. Next Steps with date

**Present 3 options always. 85% pick middle. 20% upgrade within 6 months.**

### Sales Metrics
- Calls/day per rep: 20-30
- Emails/day per rep: 50-100
- Discovery meetings/week: 3-5
- Proposals/month: 4-6
- Deals/month per rep: 1-2
- Average deal size (Year 1): ₹15-20 Lakh
- Pipeline rule: Keep 10x pipeline vs monthly target

- Commission: 15-20% of deal value
- Offer: Free 1-day DPDP training + co-branded tools + marketing support
- Target: 500 CA partner firms in 12 months
- Revenue: 500 firms × 2 referrals/yr × ₹3L = ₹30 Crore/year

### Channel Type 2: Law Firms (Premium Referrals)
- Revenue share: 10-15%
- They advise legal, you implement tech
- Target Tier 2/3 city firms (Tier 1 already with Big 4)
- Target: 200 law firm partners in 18 months

### Channel Type 3: Cybersecurity Firms
- Joint go-to-market: "Security + DPDP Bundle"
- Revenue share: 20-25%
- White-label option available
- Target: 50 cybersecurity firm partners

### Channel Type 4: HR Tech Integration
- API integration — your compliance module inside HR platforms
- Target: Darwinbox, Keka, GreytHR, Zoho People
- One partnership = access to thousands of company clients
- Revenue share: 30% recurring

### Channel Math
- 500 CA + 200 law + 50 cyber + 3 HR integrations = 1,750 new clients/year
- At ₹3L average → ₹52 Crore/year from channel alone

## Pricing Strategy

### The Golden Rule
Price against RISK, not cost.
"Your DPDP exposure = ₹50 Crore. Our service = ₹5 Lakh. 100x ROI on risk reduction."

### Price Architecture
| Service | SME | Mid-Market | Enterprise |
|---------|-----|-----------|-----------|
| Assessment | ₹75K | ₹3-5L | ₹8-15L |
| Implementation | ₹2-5L | ₹8-20L | ₹25-75L |
| DPO-as-a-Service | ₹50K/yr | ₹1.5L/yr | ₹5-15L/yr |
| SaaS Platform | ₹49,999/yr | ₹1,99,999/yr | ₹5-15L/yr |
| CDPP Certification | ₹12,000 | — | Bulk ₹9K each |

### Pricing Rules
- Always present 3 options (A/B/C)
- Annual plans with 2-month free for commitment
- Never discount below 20%
- "Early bird" creates urgency
