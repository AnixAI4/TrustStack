## The One-Page Summary

### What is DPDP?
India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. India's GDPR. Penalties up to ₹250 Crore.
Rules being notified in 2025-26. Enforcement begins 2026. YOU HAVE 12-18 MONTHS.

### Who Must Comply?
Every company with digital operations + Indian users. 10-15 Lakh companies in India.
Foreign companies with Indian users also covered. No industry exempt.
