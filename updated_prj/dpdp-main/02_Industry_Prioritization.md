# Industry Prioritization for DPDP Business

## The 4-Factor Scoring Framework

- Factor 1: DATA VOLUME & SENSITIVITY — How much personal data do they hold?
- Factor 2: REGULATORY URGENCY — Are they already on government radar?
- Factor 3: ABILITY TO PAY — Do they have budget for compliance?
- Factor 4: MARKET SIZE IN INDIA — How many companies in this industry?

Each industry scored 1-5 per factor. Total = Priority Score (max 20).

---

## TIER 1 — Strike First (Highest ROI, Fastest Revenue)

### Priority 1: BFSI (Score: 20/20)
- Data: KYC, Aadhaar, PAN, transactions, credit scores
- Regulated by: RBI, SEBI, IRDAI + DPDP
- Market: 12 PSU banks, 21 private banks, 1500+ NBFCs, 9000+ cooperatives
- Why first: Compliance DNA already exists, fastest deal closure

### Priority 2: Healthcare & Hospitals (Score: 19/20)
- Data: Medical records, Aadhaar-linked health IDs, ABDM data
- Regulated by: Health Ministry + ABDM + DPDP
- Market: 70,000+ hospitals, 600+ medical colleges, health-tech startups
- Why second: Health data = most sensitive, CoWIN leak precedent, CEOs scared

### Priority 3: Edtech & Education (Score: 18/20)
- Data: Children's data — DPDP's strictest category (Under 18)
- Regulated by: DPDP children provisions (₹200 Cr penalty)
- Market: 1.5M schools, 50,000+ colleges, 4000+ edtech startups
- Why third: Every edtech currently violating children's data provisions
