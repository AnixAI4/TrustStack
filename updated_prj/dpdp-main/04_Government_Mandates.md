# Step 3: Government Mandates — The Full Regulatory Stack

## The 5-Floor Compliance Building

- Floor 5: DPDP Act 2023 — overarching data protection law
- Floor 4: Sector Regulators — RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, TRAI, UGC
- Floor 3: IT Act 2000 + IT Rules 2011 + CERT-In Directions 2022
- Floor 2: State Government Mandates

Companies must comply with ALL floors simultaneously.

---

## Floor 5: DPDP Act — Core Central Mandates

### Every Data Fiduciary Must:
1. Give clear notice in all 22 scheduled languages
2. Obtain free, informed, specific consent
3. Use data only for stated purpose
4. Collect only what is strictly necessary
5. Keep data accurate and updated
6. Define and publish data retention periods — delete when done
7. Implement encryption, access controls, audit logs
8. Notify Data Protection Board + affected individuals of any breach
9. Appoint grievance officer, respond to complaints
10. Verify age + get parental consent for Under-18 data

### Significant Data Fiduciaries (SDFs) Must Also:
- Appoint Data Protection Officer (DPO) based in India
- Conduct Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIA)
- Submit to periodic independent audits
- Provide algorithmic transparency
- Comply with stricter data localisation rules

## Floor 4: Sector Regulator Mandates

### RBI (BFSI)
- Master Direction on IT Framework — Board-approved policies mandatory
- Cybersecurity Framework — 24x7 SOC, incident response
- Digital Lending Guidelines 2022 — no access to contacts/camera/mic, India-only storage
- Payment Aggregator Guidelines — data localisation, annual security audit
- New with DPDP: consent architecture, right to erasure, children's account rules

### SEBI (Capital Markets)
- Cybersecurity Circular 2023 + Cyber Resilience Framework
- KYC Registration Agency data standards
- New with DPDP: investor consent management, trading data profiling restrictions
- KEY CONFLICT: SEBI 8-year retention vs DPDP deletion requirement

### IRDAI (Insurance)
- Information and Cyber Security Guidelines 2023
- Health Insurance data portability guidelines
- New with DPDP: policyholder consent, TPA processor agreements, AI underwriting transparency

### TRAI (Telecom)
- Do Not Disturb (DND) registry
- New with DPDP: subscriber consent at scale, location data controls, OTT platform coverage

### UGC + Education Ministry
- New with DPDP: parental consent for Under-18, no behavioral advertising, academic data retention policy

## Floor 3: IT Act + CERT-In (Already in Force)

- IT Act Section 43A — compensation for failure to protect data
- IT Rules 2011 SPDI Rules — still valid until DPDP Rules supersede
- CERT-In Directions 2022:
  - Report incidents within 6 HOURS (already in force)
  - Maintain logs for 180 days
  - Mandatory KYC for VPN users
  - Annual cybersecurity audit

## Critical Compliance Conflicts (Your Consulting Goldmine)

| Conflict | Law 1 | Law 2 | Resolution Needed |
|----------|-------|-------|------------------|
| Retention vs Deletion | DPDP: delete when done | PMLA: 10 yrs, SEBI: 8 yrs | Legal basis + tiered retention policy |
| Consent vs Legal Obligation | DPDP: need consent | Courts/Police: can subpoena | Legal obligation processing basis |
| Data Localisation | RBI: India only | DPDP: restricted countries list | Hybrid cloud architecture |
| Right to Erasure vs Fraud | DPDP: user can demand deletion | Banks: must keep fraud records | Legitimate interest documentation |
| Children's Age Verification | DPDP: parental consent | Schools: can't verify digitally | DigiLocker-based consent |

## Data Protection Board of India

- Receives and investigates complaints
- Conducts suo motu inquiries
- Imposes penalties up to ₹250 Crore
- Can block non-compliant entities from processing data
- Digital-first — complaints filed online
- AI-assisted complaint management

---

## Government/PSU Compliance

- PSUs like SBI, LIC, ONGC, AIIMS — COVERED under DPDP
- SBI: data of 500M+ customers
- LIC: 300M+ policyholders
- PSU contracts: ₹5-50 Crore each
- Government sovereign functions (law enforcement, national security) — EXEMPT
