## Intent

One page template + one content file per industry, fed by the industry pack file (Step 5 Q6) and the
marketing kits in `DPDP_revised/marketing material/`. BFSI, Healthcare and University first.

## Must not be lost when promoted
- No free scanner, no health check (Aniket, 28 Oct 2026).
- Two calls to action only: Book a briefing · Request a readiness assessment.
- The lead form uses TrustStack's own notice and consent; no tracking without consent.
- The chatbot (F-41) on the guarded AI gateway: answers from the Act and Rules with citations, "general
  information, not legal advice", no personal data, rate limit and cost cap — **live only after A1**.
- Never "compliant", "certified" or "Consent Manager" (analysis §7.2).
