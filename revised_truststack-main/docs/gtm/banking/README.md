## Key facts to carry forward

- **Deadlines:** RBI Responsible Business Conduct (Second Amendment) Directions 2026, explicit consent per product, from 1 Jan 2027. DPDP Rules 3 and 5–16 from 13 May 2027. Consent Manager registration from 13 Nov 2026.
- **RBI 2026 instruments:** Advisory 3/2026 on customer data protection (25 Mar 2026); Draft Data Governance Guidance (15 Jul 2026); Cybersecurity and Digital Payment Security Directions (31 Jul 2026, 6-hour reporting through DAKSH).
- **Market:** about 11,400 RBI-regulated lenders (139 commercial banks, 1,842 cooperative banks, 9,382 NBFCs at end-March 2025). The sweet spot is about 800 mid-sized lenders.
- **Tenders need a banking reference:** 3–5 years in business, about ₹2 crore turnover and a prior BFSI privacy project. So the founding pilot must be paid, with a purchase order and a completion letter.
- **Channels:** FIDC (RBI-recognised NBFC self-regulatory body, Oct 2025) and NUCFDC (urban cooperative bank umbrella body).
- **Product truth:** TrustStack shows only the DPDP 72-hour breach clock today. RBI and CERT-In 6-hour clocks, MFA (Slice 13) and the in-premises edition (Slice 15) are not built. Never demo or claim them.
