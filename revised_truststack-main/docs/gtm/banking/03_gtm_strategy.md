### The logic

- **Below the global suites, above the do-it-yourself tools.** OneTrust is quoted at about ₹40–50 lakh a year before implementation. Indian tool-and-template offers start at about ₹4 lakh plus ₹2 lakh a year (ComplyDP's published programme price). Small consultancies quote ₹1.5–4 lakh with no platform.

- **Against the risk.** One unreported breach can draw a penalty of up to ₹200 crore. RBI's new consent rules make mis-selling refundable with compensation. Our full first-year cost for a mid-sized lender is about ₹25–35 lakh.

- **Land, then expand.** The subscription is where the long-term value sits, so we price services to win and keep the platform renewing. Price a fixed scope, never open-ended time and materials.

- **Volume for associations.** Small-lender kit at ₹1.5 lakh for 25+ institutions and ₹1 lakh for 100+, with co-branding for the association.

- **Public tenders.** These are scored on quality and cost; quote the Enterprise bands with a partner and expect pressure on price.

**Payment terms:** services 40% on purchase order, 40% on the assessment report, 20% on the Board pack; platform yearly in advance.

## The founding pilot

A paid, fixed-scope, 10-week Assess & Fix at one institution, starting 16 Nov 2026 and ending by 29 Jan 2027. It must be paid. Tenders accept only a purchase order plus a satisfactory completion letter as proof of experience (IPPB's RFP asks for exactly this), and a paid pilot gets management attention.

### Scope

- Three to five customer journeys chosen with the sponsor, for example: digital personal loan, secured loan (gold or home), deposits and KYC, collections, grievances.

- The whole institution's governance, breach readiness, vendor list and retention, at policy level.

- No customer records are needed. We work with process information, policies, system and vendor lists, and sample notices and screens.

### Week by week

| Weeks | Dates | What happens | Output |
|----|----|----|----|
| 1 | 16–20 Nov | Kick-off with sponsor and steering committee; document request; TrustStack workspace set up with the BFSI pack; journeys chosen | Workplan, owners (RACI) |
| 2–3 | 23 Nov–4 Dec | Workshops per journey; data map; vendor and lending-partner list; evidence collection | Starter ROPA, vendor register |
| 4 | 7–11 Dec | **RBI consent rules check:** per-product consent, "No" by default, records kept, against the 1 Jan 2027 rules | Consent fix list, quick wins before 1 Jan |
| 5–6 | 14–24 Dec | Scoring across 16 areas with RBI overlay; gap register ranked by penalty risk; interim readout to sponsor | Baseline score, gap list, 30-60-90 plan |
| 7–9 | 4–22 Jan | Fix sprint: notices for the chosen journeys, consent journeys, retention schedule, breach playbook and a 72-hour tabletop drill, vendor clauses, rights and grievance procedure | Working documents, drill report |
| 10 | 25–29 Jan | Reassessment of the fixed areas; Board pack and presentation; anonymised findings agreed for the RBI note | Before-and-after score, Board pack, completion letter |

### How we will know it worked

- A baseline score and a reassessed score, with every change backed by evidence.

- All critical (P0) gaps in the chosen journeys closed or owned with dates.

- ROPA for the chosen journeys at least 80% complete on TrustStack's completeness measure.

- Consent journeys meet RBI's rules before 1 Jan 2027.

- A breach tabletop run end to end inside 72 hours.

- Board pack presented; completion letter and reference agreed.

## The RBI play

Go to RBI with evidence and a public good, not a product. Send a short, neutral note after the pilot, through the right channel, offering a reusable framework the sector can adopt.

**What we propose to RBI (three asks, all optional for RBI):**

1.  **A common DPDP-and-RBI control baseline.** We contribute an open, citation-backed map linking each DPDP duty to the RBI directions that already touch it (Advisory 3/2026, the consent rules, digital lending, cyber reporting, KYC retention). FIDC, NUCFDC or IBA could adopt it as a voluntary standard for members.

2.  **A capacity programme for small lenders.** About 10,000 small NBFCs and cooperative banks need affordable help before 13 May 2027. A guided kit delivered through the self-regulatory and umbrella bodies lifts the floor of the sector.

3.  **Shared learning from the pilot.** Anonymised findings show what a real lender's gaps look like, such as where consent, retention and vendor control break. That can inform RBI's supervisory focus.

**What we do not ask for:** endorsement, a mandate to use TrustStack, or any preferential treatment.

**Channel:** the executive forwards it, ideally with FIDC (NBFC self-regulatory body) or NUCFDC (cooperative banks) as co-signatory. RBI's supervision side issued Advisory 3/2026, so that is the likely audience; the executive will know the right office.

**Timing:**

- **Now (optional):** a one-page note of intent, describing the pilot and the plan to share findings. It shows good faith and lets RBI say if it would welcome the findings.

- **February 2027:** the full readiness note, with anonymised pilot findings and the draft control baseline.

**Format (made in Step 4):** a two-page note, a six-slide summary, and the control baseline as an appendix. Counsel reviews it before it leaves.
