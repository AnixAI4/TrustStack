## 1. Owner Action Pack: "What do I personally owe, and by when?"

PDF, one per owner, each owner with their own version numbers. Carries
D-011's caveat only: it records no exposure snapshot, because owners hold no
`score.calculate`.

1. **Header**: "For <owner>", as of, version, owner, generated by.
2. **At a glance**: actions owed (from the assessment AND the register),
   overdue (red when > 0), due in the next 30 days, closed. A notice if any
   of the owner's processing records is due for review or has no review date.
3. **Overdue**: one table, oldest first.
4. **Your actions, by when they are due**: one table per window (next 30
   days, 31–60, 61–90, after 90, next assessment cycle), each headed with its
   count. Every row: action, severity, where it comes from ("Assessment ·
   Notice management", "Processing register · ROPA-001 · No verifiable
   parental consent"), due date, status, and what the evidence says.
5. **Evidence to provide**: every owed action without accepted evidence,
   with what is needed ("No evidence attached", "Evidence awaiting review",
   "Evidence returned (Partial) — provide more"). Then how an action closes,
   in the platform's own words (D-056), and "Nobody reviews their own upload."
6. **Your processing register records**: code and name, status,
   completeness /100 and band, missing areas, risk, next review (red
   "overdue" or "Not scheduled").

**Who can make one:** the programme roles, for anyone; **any member, for
themselves** (decision 9). An owner downloads their own pack without
`report.download`. Nobody else's.

## 2. Regulator-Ready Evidence Pack: "If the Board asked tomorrow, can we show our evidence?"

PDF, "For an auditor, a reviewer, or a Board inquiry (s.28)". It records a
fresh exposure snapshot like the other exposure reports, and **generates its
Evidence Index (XLSX) first, from the same reads, then names it** on the
last page.

1. **Where you stand**: the stored headline, the s.37 notice and the basis,
   as in the Brief.
2. **Your evidence at a glance**: artefacts stored, accepted by a reviewer
   (Adequate or Strong), awaiting review, not linked to anything. Then why it
   matters: mitigation evidence is a s.33(2)(e) factor.
3. **Evidence by penalty head**: each head's stored status, the areas that
   evidence it directly, and its artefacts, accepted, awaiting review and
   not accepted. An artefact counts **once**, however many things it supports.
4. **Evidence by obligation area**: all sixteen Compass areas, each with the
   heads it evidences or "Enabling control", the same counts, and the
   scoring engine's evidence confidence.
5. **Processing register**: how many live records have evidence.
6. **Remediation closed, and on what basis**: closed on accepted evidence,
   closed on a reviewer's approval, still outstanding, and every closed
   action with who closed it, when and on what basis.
7. **Review status of every artefact**, and the Evidence Index's file name.
8. Both caveats verbatim, and the notes.

## 3. The three working spreadsheets

Each opens on an **About** sheet: title, organisation, as of, version, who
generated it, **D-011's caveat**, and notes on how to read it. **Values only:
no cell holds a formula or a link.** Anything a client typed that Excel could
run as a formula (starting `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`) is written with a leading
apostrophe, the same rule the CSV export has always used.

- **Gap & Action Tracker**. *Actions*: one row per action from every
  assessment and the register, with source, assessment or record, area or
  rule, severity, priority, owner (or "Unassigned"), status, due date,
  roadmap window, overdue, penalty heads, artefacts attached and accepted,
  closed on, by and on what basis, and notes. *Gaps*: one row per gap, with
  finding, fix, state and actions still owed. Filters and a frozen header row.
- **ROPA Register**: exactly the CSV export's columns (now one shared list),
  so the sheet imports straight back.
- **Evidence Index**: one row per artefact and what it supports: "Assessment
  answer" (named by area and question text, never a code), "Remediation
  action" or "Register record". An artefact linked to nothing gets one "Not
  linked" row. Each row also carries the review verdict, level, reviewer and
  date, and the penalty heads.
